# Case information — fictional test case (Texas)

All names, businesses and events below are invented for testing.

- **Client (plaintiff):** Brightline Digital LLC, a Texas limited liability company that builds websites and web software, based in Houston, Texas (Harris County).
- **Other party (defendant):** Gulf Coast Family Dental PLLC, a Texas professional limited liability company with its only office in Pasadena, Texas (Harris County).
- **Contract:** On March 2, 2026, Brightline and Gulf Coast signed a written services agreement for a new practice website and an online patient booking portal for $46,000. It sets three milestones: design approval ($11,500), portal delivery ($23,000) and launch ($11,500), each payable within 30 days of Brightline's invoice. It names no interest rate on late payments and has no attorney fee clause.
- **Payment:** Gulf Coast paid the design milestone of $11,500 on April 6, 2026 (bank record available).
- **Work:** Brightline delivered the booking portal on May 15, 2026. Gulf Coast's office manager, Teresa Villanueva, signed a written acceptance of the portal on May 20, 2026. The website launched on June 1, 2026, and Gulf Coast has used the site and portal to book patients every day since.
- **Invoices:** Brightline invoiced the portal milestone ($23,000) on May 20, 2026 and the launch milestone ($11,500) on June 2, 2026. Neither has been paid. The $34,500 balance is unpaid.
- **Dispute:** On July 8, 2026, after a reminder, Gulf Coast's owner, Dr. Alan Pierce, emailed for the first time that the portal "doesn't look the way we wanted" and that the practice "won't pay for something we didn't approve." He did not identify any defect, and the practice kept using the portal.
- **Demand:** On July 20, 2026, Brightline sent Gulf Coast a written demand for the $34,500 by certified mail and email. Gulf Coast has not paid or responded. Brightline's owner, Nadia Haddad, can sign a sworn statement of the account.
- **What the client wants:** the $34,500 balance, interest, attorney fees, and any other recovery available.
