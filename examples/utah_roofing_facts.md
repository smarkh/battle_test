# Case information — fictional test case (Utah)

All names, businesses and events below are invented for testing.

- **Client (plaintiff):** Dana Whitfield, homeowner, 1482 N Canyon View Dr, Provo, Utah (Utah County).
- **Other party (defendant):** Summit Peak Roofing LLC, a Utah limited liability company based in Orem, Utah.
- **Contract:** On March 3, 2026, Whitfield and Summit Peak signed a written contract for a full roof replacement for $24,000. Completion was due by April 30, 2026. The contract says work will be "performed in a good and workmanlike manner" and that Summit Peak will "protect the structure from weather during the work."
- **Payment:** Whitfield paid a $12,000 deposit on March 5, 2026 (bank record available).
- **Work:** Summit Peak tore off the old roof on April 14, 2026, covered part of it with tarps, and did not return. Whitfield texted the owner, Brent Kolb, on April 16, 18 and 22. On April 22 Kolb texted back: "Sorry, we're slammed, can't get back to you this month."
- **Damage:** A storm on April 24, 2026 blew the tarps loose. Water came in and damaged the ceilings, insulation and hardwood floors in two bedrooms. A water-damage contractor's written estimate for the repairs is $18,700.
- **Replacement roofer:** Whitfield hired Alpine Crest Roofing, which finished the roof on May 20, 2026 for $26,500 (invoice available).
- **Demand:** On June 2, 2026, Whitfield sent Summit Peak a written demand for the $12,000 deposit and the water-damage costs. Summit Peak has not responded.
- **What the client wants:** the deposit back, the extra cost of the replacement roofer, the water-damage repair costs, and any other damages available.
