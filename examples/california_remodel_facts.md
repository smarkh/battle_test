- **Contract:** On February 9, 2026, Natarajan and Golden Oak signed a written home improvement contract to remodel her kitchen for $58,000. Work was to start March 2, 2026 and finish by May 15, 2026. The contract lists California contractor license number 1045XXX for Golden Oak.
